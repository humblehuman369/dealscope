# DealGapIQ — The Investor's Journey
## Voiceover Script (~4.5-minute cut, v1)

**Length:** ~4:30–4:50
**Word count:** ~600 words (~130 wpm with breaks)
**Format:** Narrative VO over live screen recording (1920×1080)
**Tone:** Soft, warm, inspirational — hopeful storyteller, not presenter. A calm voice
telling you the good deals aren't gone. Zero sales energy.
**Replaces:** `vo-script-sales-demo-6min.md` as the primary cut (sales-demo voice retired)

**Pronunciation:** DealGapIQ = "Deal-Gap-EYE-Q" · BRRRR = "B-R-R-R"

**Machine-readable scenes:** `scripts/walkthrough-video/scenes.json`

---

## The spine

This is not a feature tour. It is one investor, one property, one evening — told in
second person. The chapters are the investor's decisions, not the product's screens:

> Find → See clearly → Choose → Turn a "no" into a path → Say the words → Make it yours → Begin again

Every scene should feel like encouragement. The emotional arc: quiet frustration →
clarity → possibility → confidence → hope.

---

## Direction for the talent / voice selection

Read slower and lower than feels natural. Smile gently on "They're not." and
"Not this time." Let the pauses breathe — the UI carries the proof, the voice carries
the reassurance. No announcer punch, no urgency, no competitive edge.

**ElevenLabs settings (softer than the sales demo):**
Multilingual v2 · stability **55** · similarity 75 · style **10** · speaker boost ON.
Choose a warm, unhurried voice via `ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID` — audition for gentleness
over authority.

**Do not say:** "Income Gap" · "every property is a deal" · stale free-tier counts
(use **10 free analyses/month**).

## Human read (with pause marks)

`/` short breath · `//` section beat · **Bold** = gentle emphasis

### [0:00 – 0:40] The evening every investor knows
*Visual: Homepage hero; slow cursor to the address bar.*

> Every investor knows the feeling. //
> Another evening of scrolling listings — / and nothing pencils at asking. //
> It starts to feel like the good deals are gone. //
> **They're not.** /
> Most of them are hiding inside properties that don't work — *yet*. //
> This is the story of one address, / and how it becomes a deal. //
> Paste it in. / No signup. No credit card. /
> In a few moments, you'll see the whole picture.

---

### [0:40 – 1:10] Honest numbers
*Visual: Address submit → analyzing → sources resolving.*

> So let's run one — a real property, live. //
> While it works, DealGapIQ gathers real numbers — /
> Zillow, RentCast, Redfin, Realtor.com — /
> and blends them into one honest **IQ Estimate**. //
> If a source doesn't have the answer, we simply say so. /
> Because a decision this big deserves the truth.

---

### [1:10 – 1:55] Clarity
*Visual: `/discovery` — Target Buy, Income Value, Market Price; Deal Gap bar animates.*

> And here it is — your **Verdict**. //
> Three numbers, / and suddenly everything is calmer. //
> **Target Buy** — the price where this property meets your goals. /
> **Income Value** — the most you can pay and still sleep at night. /
> **Market Price** — what the seller is asking. //
> The space between them is your **Deal Gap**. //
> Walk away, negotiate, or write the offer — /
> every one of those is a good outcome, / because now it's an *informed* one.

---

### [1:55 – 2:30] Possibility
*Visual: IQ Estimate Selector switch; six strategy snapshots scroll.*

> Every number here shows where it came from. /
> Switch the source — watch the math follow. /
> Nothing hidden. Nothing invented. //
> And maybe this property isn't the rental you imagined. /
> Maybe it's a flip. / A house hack. / Your first BRRRR. //
> Six strategies, scored side by side — /
> so the property can tell you / what it wants to be.

---

### [2:30 – 3:25] The turn
*Visual: Four Paths panel — price / capital / financing / income + Blended Plan.*

> Now — the moment that changes everything. //
> The gap is negative. /
> This is where most journeys end. / A sigh. A closed tab. Back to scrolling. //
> **Not this time.** //
> DealGapIQ opens four paths to close the gap: /
> a thoughtful price ask, / a capital ask, /
> creative financing — like seller carry, or Subject-To — /
> and re-verifying the income. //
> Then it blends the smallest asks into one fair, credible package. //
> A six-percent price cut might be a no. /
> Two percent, plus seller carry, plus verified rent — /
> that can be the same math. / And a yes. //
> The deal you almost walked past / just came back to life.

---

### [3:25 – 4:05] The words
*Visual: Pitch Script modal — who to call, opener, ask, seller benefit; Copy / Email / Print.*

> Maybe you've never made that call before. / That's okay. //
> For every path, you get the words: /
> who to call, / how to open, / what to ask for — /
> and what the seller gains by saying yes. //
> Because the best negotiations aren't battles. /
> They're two people finding the same answer. //
> Print it. Email it. Carry it on your phone. /
> You'll walk in prepared — and it shows.

---

### [4:05 – 4:40] Make it yours
*Visual: Deal Maker — sliders move; metrics recalc live.*

> When you're ready, make the deal your own. //
> In **Deal Maker**, slide the purchase price. / The down payment. / The rent. //
> Every number updates live — /
> the same math, the same record as your Verdict. //
> Shape it until it feels right. //
> And when it does, / export it to Excel /
> and take it to your lender with confidence.

---

> DealGapIQ helps you recognize it. //
> Start free — ten analyses a month, no credit card. //
