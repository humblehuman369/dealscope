# DealGapIQ — Full Application Walkthrough
## Presentation Script (v2)

**Length:** ~10–12 minutes  
**Format:** Presenter VO over screen recording (web desktop primary; mobile cutaways optional)  
**Tone:** Confident, warm, founder-demo energy — explain to a peer, don’t pitch from a stage  
**Audience:** Active residential investors analyzing 20–100 properties to close 1–3/year  

**Pronunciation:** DealGapIQ = “Deal-Gap-EYE-Q” · BRRRR = “B-R-R-R” · Proforma = “pro-FOR-muh”

**Pause marks:** `/` short breath · `//` section beat · **Bold** = emphasis

**Demo tip:** Use one real address that shows a meaningful Deal Gap and at least one viable Four Paths structure. Stay on that property for Verdict → Strategy → Deal Maker so continuity is obvious.

## Direction for the talent

Read like you’re walking a fellow investor through your own workbench over coffee. Slight smile. Trust from steadiness, not hype. Leave a half-beat after each sentence — the UI does half the work. Avoid announcer cadence and fake urgency.

**Do not say:** “Income Gap” · “every property is a deal” · stale free-tier counts (use **10 free analyses/month**) · Expo/React Native (it’s one web app on iOS/Android too)

**Close line to land:** *The price tag isn’t the deal. The structure is. We analyze. You decide.*

## ACT 2 — Discovery / Verdict (the hero) (~2:30)

### [1:30 – 2:20] The three numbers
*Visual: `/discovery` — Target Buy, Income Value, Market Price; Deal Gap bar animates.*

> This is your **Verdict**. //
> Three numbers say it all. /
> **Target Buy** — the price that hits your return thresholds. /
> **Income Value** — the max you can pay and still cash-flow positive. /
> **Market Price** — what the seller wants. //
> The **Deal Gap** between them is the focus. /
> Walk away. Negotiate. Or write the offer. /
> **We show you options.**

*On-screen text:* Target Buy · Income Value · Market Price → Deal Gap = the focus

## ACT 3 — Four Paths (the moat) (~2:00)

### [3:30 – 4:30] When it doesn’t pencil
*Visual: Four Paths panel — price / capital / financing / income + Blended Plan.*

> Here’s the part most tools never reach. //
> A negative gap isn’t a dead deal. /
> It’s a **structure** problem. //
> DealGapIQ builds four paths to close the gap: /
> a price ask, /
> a capital ask, /
> financing — Subject-To, seller carry, creative structures — /
> income re-verification, /
> and a **Blended Plan** that combines smaller asks into one credible package. //
> A six-percent price cut can be a no. /
> Two percent plus seller carry plus verified rent / can be the same math — and a yes.

---

### [4:30 – 5:30] Pitch script
*Visual: Open Pitch Script modal — who to call, frame, opener, ask, seller benefit. Show Copy / Email / Print.*

> And we don’t stop at the spreadsheet. //
> For each path you get a **pitch script**: /
> who to call, /
> how to frame it, /
> the opener, /
> the ask, /
> and what’s in it for the seller. //
> Print it. Email it. Copy it to your phone before you dial. //
> This is the synthesis layer — /
> from “does it pencil?” / to “how do I actually get this under contract?”

*On-screen text:* That’s where most tools stop. DealGapIQ keeps going.

### [6:20 – 7:10] Deal Maker
*Visual: Deal Maker — drag purchase price / down payment / rent; metrics update live. Optional Excel export.*

> Every metric recalculates live — /
