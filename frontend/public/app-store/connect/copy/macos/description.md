```
See every US listing through an investor’s lens — on your Mac. DealGapIQ pre-scores any property DEAL, MAYBE, or PASS and shows the DealGap: how far the asking price is from the price that actually makes the deal work.

Enter an address and get an investor-grade analysis in seconds on a wide desktop canvas:

• The DealGap — how far the asking price is from the income-supported value
• Target Buy Price — the price to aim for to hit positive cash flow
• Income Value — the break-even price where rent covers all costs
• IQ Estimates — value and rent cross-referenced from Zillow, RentCast, Redfin, Realtor.com, and county records

• Key Metrics — Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash Return, DSCR, and monthly cash flow
• DealMaker — adjust purchase price, financing, and expenses with live metric updates
• Reports — full property and appraisal reports, plus an editable Excel worksheet

BUILT FOR THE DESKTOP
Model scenarios side-by-side, review comps without shrinking windows, and underwrite faster on a keyboard-first Mac experience. Same investor engine as iPhone and iPad — optimized for a big screen.

COVERAGE BEYOND THE MLS
```
