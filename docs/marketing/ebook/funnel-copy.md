### Email 2 — Three numbers (Day 2)

**Subject:** Hours of analysis → three numbers

**Body:**

Most new investors drown in metrics.

You only need three to avoid a bad first purchase:

1. **Income Value** — the ceiling where income stops supporting the deal  
2. **Target Buy** — that ceiling after *your* margin  
3. **Deal Gap** — how far asking sits from Target Buy  

If you can’t say all three out loud for a listing, you’re still in the marketing lens.

### Email 3 — Bands + mistakes (Day 4)

**Subject:** A 14% Deal Gap isn’t a verdict — it’s a workload

**Body:**

Quick bands from the book:

- **0–5%** — normal negotiation / small tweak territory  
- **5–15%** — real work (price, capital, income check, or structure)  
- **15%+** — hard mode: clear path, new strategy, or pass  

Phantom deals usually come from fabricated rent, ignored insurance, or treating list price as value.

**Challenge:** Pick a listing you almost liked. Which band is it in — really?

### Email 4 — Four Paths (Day 6)

**Subject:** The price tag isn’t the deal. The structure is.

**Body:**

When the Deal Gap is positive, you have four levers:

1. **Price** — negotiate toward Target Buy  
2. **Capital** — change the stack (carefully)  
3. **Income re-verify** — fix the inputs  
4. **Structure** — seller financing and creative paths  

Most beginners only try #1 — then conclude “nothing works.”

**Try this:** Assume a 12% gap. Which path would you try first, and why?

DealGapIQ surfaces paths on each verdict so you’re not stuck at “cash flow negative.”
