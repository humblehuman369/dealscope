Reading the Deal Gap

Effort bands for new investors — not false precision

0–5% 5–15% 15%+
Closer Real work Hard mode

0–5% — Close enough

Normal negotiation or a small structure tweak may work. Still verify rent, insurance, and repairs — a thin gap with fantasy inputs is still a phantom deal.

5–15% — Real work

Price alone may not be enough. Use the Four Paths: price, capital, income re-verify, or structure. Name your first path before you tour again.

15%+ — Hard mode

Need a clear path, a different strategy (house hack, wholesale, BRRRR…), or a pass. Big gaps can close with motivation + structure — they rarely close on hope.

Formula: Deal Gap % = (Asking Price − Target Buy) / Asking Price × 100. Positive = asking above Target Buy. The price tag isn’t the deal — the structure is.
