## The pitch

Under 150 words, built on the Foreclosure.com → DealGapIQ story. Every claim is from `docs/brand/content-source-of-truth.md`. Swap the bracketed line for the show.

---

**Subject:** Guest idea: the Foreclosure.com founder on why asking price is the wrong question

Hi [host],

[One sentence naming a recent episode on the show and what it covered.]

I'm Brad Geisen. I founded Foreclosure.com, which still powers BiggerPockets' foreclosure search, and my team built HomePath.com for Fannie Mae and HomeSteps.com for Freddie Mac. That work goes back to a 1991 HUD pilot, and it taught me one thing: listing sites tell you the asking price, calculators tell you whether a deal works at that price, and neither tells you the price at which it does work.

That is what I built DealGapIQ to answer. It shows the gap between a property's asking price and the price at which it works for an investor, then gives four paths plus a Blend to close that gap. Free to start.

Happy to talk through how the gap math works, the four paths, and what I got wrong along the way. My book, The Deal Gap, covers the same ground.

Brad Geisen, Founder and CEO of DealGapIQ. Founded Foreclosure.com, built HomePath.com for Fannie Mae and HomeSteps.com for Freddie Mac. Author of The Deal Gap.
Press kit: https://dealgapiq.com/press
