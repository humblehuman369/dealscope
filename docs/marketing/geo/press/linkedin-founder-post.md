I spent most of my career building the pricing tools banks used on foreclosures. Foreclosure.com, HomePath for Fannie Mae, HomeSteps for Freddie Mac. As an investor I kept hitting the same wall.

Listing sites tell you the asking price. Calculators tell you whether a deal works at that price. Neither tells you the price at which it does work, or how to get there.

That difference is the deal gap. A Lake Worth, FL listing asks $457,100. At 20% down the numbers say pay $428,000. The gap is -6.4%, about $29,000. Once you know that number, "is this a deal?" becomes "which path closes $29,000?" Price, Income, Terms, Equity, or a Blend of them.
