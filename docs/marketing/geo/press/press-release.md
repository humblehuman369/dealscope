## DealGapIQ Launches Publicly: Free Real Estate Deal Analysis Tool Shows Investors the Gap Between a Property's Price and Its Investor Value

**BOCA RATON, Fla., [Month Day, 2026]** — DealGapIQ, founded by Foreclosure.com founder Brad Geisen, publicly launched in August 2026 as a free real estate deal analysis tool that shows investors the gap between a property's price and its investor value, and four paths plus a Blend to close it.

DealGapIQ is a real estate investment analysis tool that shows the gap between a property's asking price and the price at which it works for an investor, then gives four paths plus a Blend to close that gap. It analyzes six strategies across every U.S. market in under 60 seconds, starts free, and Pro costs $34.99 a month.

**What a deal gap is**

A deal gap is the difference between what a seller is asking and the most an investor can pay and still hit their return target. Take 1014-16 N J St in Lake Worth, Florida. It lists at $457,100. At 20 percent down, the numbers say an investor should pay $428,000. The deal gap is -6.4 percent, or about $29,000. A negative gap means the list price is above the target buy price.

Listing sites report the asking price. Calculators report whether a deal works at that price. DealGapIQ reports the price at which it does work, how far the listing is from that number, and how to get there. Every discovery scores the address against six strategies: long-term rental, short-term rental, BRRRR, fix and flip, house hack, and wholesale. It then returns four paths plus a Blend to close the gap: Price, Income, Terms and Equity, and a Blend that combines them, each with an editable worksheet and a negotiation script. Valuations come from five sources, including Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com and RentCast, shown side by side.

**Pricing and availability**

The free plan is $0 with no credit card and includes three discoveries a month, the full analysis, and the negotiation scripts. Pro is $34.99 a month, or $29.17 a month billed annually, with a 7-day trial that also needs no card. Pro adds directories of 2,812 verified cash buyers and 484 hard money lenders, plus comps, Excel proformas and PDF reports. DealGapIQ runs on the web at dealgapiq.com, on iOS and macOS (App Store ID 6759636866), and on Android through Google Play. The beta opened in January 2026; updates ship weekly.
