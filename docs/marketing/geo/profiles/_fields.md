## Longer "About" paragraph (when a field allows more)

DealGapIQ is a real estate investment analysis tool that shows the gap between a property's asking price and the price at which it works for an investor, then gives four paths plus a Blend to close that gap. It analyzes six strategies across every U.S. market in under 60 seconds, starts free, and Pro costs $34.99 a month.

Every discovery scores an address, on-market or off-market, against long-term rental, short-term rental, BRRRR, fix and flip, house hack, and wholesale, and reports a target buy price and the deal gap as a percentage and a dollar amount. The four paths, Price, Income, Terms and Equity, plus a Blend, each come with an editable worksheet and a negotiation script. Valuations come from five sources, including Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com and RentCast, shown side by side. Pro adds directories of 2,812 verified cash buyers and 484 hard money lenders, comps, Excel proformas and PDF reports.

DealGapIQ is made by InvestIQ LLC in Boca Raton, Florida. It launched in beta in January 2026 and publicly in August 2026, and ships updates weekly. Brad Geisen, Founder and CEO of DealGapIQ. Founded Foreclosure.com, built HomePath.com for Fannie Mae and HomeSteps.com for Freddie Mac. Author of The Deal Gap.
