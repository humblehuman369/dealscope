## First comment (maker, first person)

Hi Product Hunt, I'm Brad, founder of DealGapIQ.

I founded Foreclosure.com and my team built HomePath.com for Fannie Mae and HomeSteps.com for Freddie Mac. For most of my career I built the pricing tools banks used on foreclosures. As an investor I kept hitting the same wall: listing sites tell you the asking price, calculators tell you whether a deal works at that price, and neither tells you the price at which it does work or how to get there.

DealGapIQ is a real estate investment analysis tool that shows the gap between a property's asking price and the price at which it works for an investor, then gives four paths plus a Blend to close that gap. It analyzes six strategies across every U.S. market in under 60 seconds, starts free, and Pro costs $34.99 a month.

A real example: 1014-16 N J St in Lake Worth, FL lists at $457,100. At 20% down the numbers say an investor should pay $428,000. The deal gap is -6.4%, about $29,000. The four paths (Price, Income, Terms, Equity) plus a Blend each come with an editable worksheet and a negotiation script, so the question becomes "which path closes $29,000?" rather than "is this a deal?"

What's free: 3 discoveries a month, the full analysis and the scripts, no card. Pro ($34.99/mo or $29.17/mo billed annually, 7-day trial, no card) adds directories of 2,812 verified cash buyers and 484 hard money lenders, comps, Excel proformas and PDF reports.

We launched in beta in January 2026, publicly in August 2026, and ship updates weekly. Web, iOS, macOS and Android.

I'd love to hear which listing you run first and what the gap came back as. I'll be here all day answering questions.
