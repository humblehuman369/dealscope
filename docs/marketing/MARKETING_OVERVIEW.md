## 1. Company Introduction

**The price tag isn't the deal. The structure is.**

Every investor knows the feeling. You pull up a listing, run the numbers, and the math says no. So you close the tab and keep scrolling. Thirty listings later you've found one candidate, rebuilt the same spreadsheet thirty times, and the weekend is gone.

DealGapIQ was built by an investor who got tired of that loop. Founder Brad Geisen spent his career on the acquisition side of residential real estate, including Foreclosure.com and work on Fannie Mae's HomePath and Freddie Mac's HomeSteps programs, and wrote *The Deal Gap* on the idea that runs through the entire product: most "dead" listings aren't dead. They're mispriced for the terms on the table, and a different structure closes them.

DealGapIQ turns any US address into a 15-second Discovery verdict, four pre-built ways to close the gap between asking price and what actually pencils, and the negotiation script for each one. It runs on the web and as a native iOS and Android app, and the same six-strategy math engine powers all three.

Listing sites end at price. Investor calculators end at cash flow. **That's where most tools stop. DealGapIQ keeps going.**

## 2. Service Portfolio

### Discovery: the 15-second verdict
Paste an address, a Zillow link, or drop a pin. DealGapIQ pulls valuation and rent data from six live sources (Zillow, RentCast, Redfin, Realtor.com, AirROI for short-term rental revenue, and public records), blends them into a proprietary **IQ Estimate**, and returns three numbers every investor needs before they pick up the phone:

- **Income Value** — the highest price you can pay before cash flow turns negative.
- **Target Buy** — the price that hits *your* return target, not a generic one.
- **Deal Gap** — the distance between asking and Target Buy. A −6.4% gap on a $457,000 listing means the seller needs to meet you at $428,000, or the structure needs to change.

Every verdict comes with a plain-English explanation and a source-by-source breakdown. Switch the source, watch the math change. If a source has no data, the field says *Unavailable*. Nothing is fabricated.

### Six strategy engines, one address
Discovery ranks the property across all six residential strategies at once, then lets you drill into the full P&L for any of them:

| Strategy | What DealGapIQ models |
|---|---|
| Long-Term Rental | Cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, DSCR, 10-year proforma |
| Short-Term Rental | ADR and occupancy-driven revenue, seasonality, management load |
| BRRRR | Rehab budget, ARV, refinance cash-out, capital left in deal |
| Fix & Flip | ARV, holding costs, profit and margin at exit |
| House Hack | FHA / owner-occupant financing, offset living cost |
| Wholesale | Assignment fee math, end-buyer MAO |

### Four Paths: offer structures that close the gap
This is the layer no calculator ships. When the math doesn't work at list price and standard terms, DealGapIQ builds four alternative offers:

1. **Income Uplift** — re-verify rent and revenue assumptions the listing understates.
2. **Realistic Ask** — the price cut that actually pencils, with the justification behind it.
3. **Capital-Heavy** — more down, better terms, a different debt structure.
4. **Blended Creative Plan** — several smaller asks that add up to the same result: a modest price reduction, a seller-carry second, a rate buydown, a subject-to or assumable mortgage.

A 6% price cut is a no. A 2% cut plus a seller-carry second plus verified rent is the same math, three smaller asks, and a yes.

### Negotiation Playbook and LOI generation
Each path ships with a pitch script: who to call, the frame, the opener, the ask, and what's in it for the seller. Print it, copy it, take it into the conversation. Generate a Letter of Intent from the structure you chose.

### Deal Maker workbench
The full underwriting worksheet, pre-loaded with the path you picked. Edit every assumption, stress-test vacancy, rates, and rehab overruns, run sensitivity tables, and view a 10-year proforma. Your own default assumptions (target returns, financing type, risk posture) flow into every analysis automatically.

### Price Intel and comps
Sale and rental comparables, market consensus across sources, and state and regional adjustments for vacancy, appreciation, and taxes so a Cleveland duplex and a Tampa duplex aren't judged by the same yardstick.

### Map Search
Hunt by neighborhood instead of by address. Filter live listings on an interactive map, including owner-tenure signals that surface likely-motivated sellers, then run Discovery on anything that looks interesting.

### Deal pipeline, tasks, and track record
Move deals through a kanban pipeline from *Analyzing* to *Closed*, attach tasks, documents, contacts, and a budget-vs-actual tracker per deal, compare saved deals side by side, and keep a running record of sold and held properties.

### Gap Alerts
Save a property and DealGapIQ watches the list price daily. When a price drop moves the Deal Gap, you get a push notification, so the seller who wouldn't take your number in March finds you ready in May.

### Investor directories (Pro)
Two curated, searchable directories built for execution, not lead-gen spam: a **Cash Buyer Directory** for wholesalers who need a buyer for the contract they just locked up, and a **Hard Money Lender Directory** for the flip or BRRRR that needs fast capital. Search, filter, export.

### Reports and exports (Pro)
Printable deal reports plus PDF and Excel exports for lenders, partners, and due diligence files. An AI-drafted deal memo summarizes the thesis so you don't write it from scratch.

## 3. Unique Value Propositions

**Streamlines the decision.** Address in, verdict out, in about 15 seconds. Thirty properties take thirty minutes, not thirty weekends. The Deal Gap tells you instantly whether to keep going or move on.

**Sharpens the number.** The IQ Estimate averages multiple independent valuation and rent sources and drops outliers automatically, so a single inflated Zestimate or a stale AVM doesn't become your offer price. Every input shows its provenance.

**Shortens the distance from listing to offer.** Most tools stop at "doesn't pencil." DealGapIQ hands you four written offers and the script to pitch each one. The time you used to spend deciding *whether* to make an offer becomes time spent making it.

**Protects the return.** Target Buy is anchored to *your* return, and Income Value marks the ceiling where cash flow turns negative. You never pay for a property that only works on someone else's assumptions.

**Finds leverage others miss.** We don't claim every property is a deal. We claim every property has more leverage than the asking price suggests, and we show you exactly where it is.

## 4. Target Audience Alignment

### The first-time investor
**Challenge:** Sees a negative Deal Gap and concludes there are no deals. Doesn't yet know that a seller-carry second or an FHA house-hack changes the math.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** Plain-English verdicts, a guided onboarding that captures experience level, budget, and target returns, and Four Paths that teach structure by showing it on a real property. The Free tier covers ten full analyses a month with no credit card, enough to learn the rhythm before the first offer.

### The active investor (1–50 doors)
**Challenge:** Analyzes 20–100 properties to close one to three a year. Analysis fatigue is the real bottleneck; the spreadsheet is accurate but slow.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** Unlimited analyses, personal default assumptions that load into every deal, stress tests and sensitivity in the Deal Maker, a pipeline to keep twenty live opportunities straight, and Gap Alerts so a saved property re-enters the funnel the day its price drops.

### The wholesaler
**Challenge:** Needs a fast verdict on off-market leads, a defensible MAO, and a buyer before the contract expires.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** Off-market addresses analyze the same way listed ones do. The Wholesale strategy calculates assignment fees against end-buyer math, and the Cash Buyer Directory puts disposition contacts one search away.

### The house hacker
**Challenge:** First purchase, owner-occupant financing, needs to know whether the other units cover the mortgage.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** The House Hack strategy models FHA and VA scenarios, and Discovery ranks it against a straight rental so the buyer sees which path their budget actually supports.

### The cold-market buy-and-hold investor
**Challenge:** Retail listings rarely cash-flow at list price and conventional terms. Deals get constructed or they don't happen.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** The Blended Creative Plan is built for exactly this market, combining a modest price cut, seller financing, and re-verified income into a single credible offer with a script to present it.

### Coaches and educators (channel partners)
**Challenge:** Curricula teach creative finance in the abstract; students freeze at the pitch.
**How DealGapIQ helps:** A live tool that proves the lesson on a student's own target property, with the script they'll use on the call.

## 5. Competitive Advantages

**The synthesis layer.** Listing sites find properties. Calculators run cash flow. Only DealGapIQ takes the verdict the next two steps: into structured offers and into the words that pitch them. That layer, the Four Paths and Negotiation Playbook, does not exist in DealCheck, Mashvisor, PropStream, or BiggerPockets' calculators.

**Proprietary multi-source valuation.** The IQ Estimate blends Zillow, RentCast, Redfin, and Realtor.com valuations with RentCast and Zillow rent data and AirROI short-term rental revenue, filtering outliers with a median-band rule. Single-source tools inherit single-source error.

**Creative-finance modeling built in.** Subject-to, seller carrybacks, 0% seconds, rate buydowns, assumable mortgages, and FHA house-hacks are first-class inputs, not workarounds in a notes field.

**Radical transparency.** Every metric shows its source. Missing data is labeled *Unavailable*, never estimated. A public methodology page explains how the verdict is computed. Investors trust receipts, not vibes.

**Investor-built, not analyst-built.** The product encodes decades of acquisition experience across foreclosure, GSE disposition, and private investing. The judgment is in the defaults, the regional adjustments, and the offer structures, so the tool thinks like a buyer, not a spreadsheet.

**One product, every screen.** Full feature parity across web, iOS, and Android. Run a verdict from the driveway, finish the worksheet at the desk.

**Execution directories, not lead lists.** Curated cash buyer and hard money lender directories are included in Pro, so the deal you just structured has a buyer and a lender in the same app.

**Honest pricing.** Free forever for ten analyses a month with no credit card. Pro at $34.99/month or $349.99/year (about $29.17/month), with a 7-day free trial. Directories and exports unlock with the first payment.
